Claim

Someone launched a token for you?
Come get your share.

If a token was deployed against your X handle, a slice of its supply is sitting in escrow with your name on it. Verifying takes about a minute. (Demo flow — the buttons below simulate each step.)

1
Sign in with X Prove you control the handle the token was launched for.
2
Connect your wallet This is where your escrowed supply will land.
3
Claim your supply One transaction. The vault opens, and the tokens are yours.

Good to know

  1. Claims never expire — your supply waits as long as it takes.
  2. Verification matches your X account to the handle named at deploy. Close spellings don't count.
  3. Claiming costs only the network fee. SupplyFun takes nothing from the creator's share.
  4. Everything is inspectable on-chain before you touch anything.
