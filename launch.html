What happens when you deploy

  1. The token mints with your chosen name and ticker.
  2. The creator's share moves straight into an on-chain escrow vault.
  3. Seed liquidity opens; deeper tranches unlock at market-cap milestones.
  4. The creator gets notified they have supply waiting — claimable any time with an X sign-in.
